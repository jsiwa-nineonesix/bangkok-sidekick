## 💳 Paying: what works where

| System | Contactless credit/debit card | Notes |
|--------|------------------------------|-------|
| **MRT (Blue & Purple Line)** | ✅ **Yes, tap Visa/Mastercard directly at the gate** (also works with Apple/Google Pay on the same card) | Use the **same card or phone to tap in and out**. Foreign-card fees may apply. Keep the card in the same wallet slot – don't tap two cards at once. |
| **BTS Skytrain** | ❌ **No direct card tap** | Use a **Rabbit card** (rechargeable, from ticket offices), a **QR ticket** (BTS/Rabbit app or machines), or a **single-journey ticket** bought with **coins/small notes** at a vending machine. Machines don't take bank notes in all stations – carry ฿10/฿5/฿1 coins or get change from the booth. |
| **Airport Rail Link** | Cash/token or its own ticket machines | Check at station |
| **Chao Phraya Express Boat** | Cash on board | Small notes needed |
| **Taxi** | Mostly cash; some cards | Use Grab instead |
| **Grab / Bolt / LINE MAN** | In-app credit card, or cash for rides | Add your card in-app **before** arriving |

🔎 Payment rules for BTS and others have been changing; verify at the station. The key tip stands: **tap card at MRT, but get a Rabbit card or QR ticket for BTS.**

### Rabbit card tip
Buy one at a BTS ticket office (deposit + stored value). The same card works on BTS and many shops. Top up at the booth or machines. For a one-week stay it's usually the easiest BTS option. 🔎

## MRT vs BTS in 3 lines

- **MRT** – underground (and partly elevated) trains, A/C, runs Blue Line through Rama 9 and Chinatown/old town.
- **BTS** – elevated trains covering Sukhumvit, Siam, Silom, Mo Chit. Different operator and ticketing from MRT.
- Changing between them requires **exiting one and tapping into the other**, so you pay two fares. Interchanges: **Sukhumvit–Asok**, **Silom–Sala Daeng**, **Chatuchak Park–Mo Chit**.

## Operating hours (approx.)

Trains run roughly **05:30–00:00**, with frequent service (every few minutes in peak hours). Avoid **07:30–09:30** and **17:00–19:30** if you can: crowded but manageable. 🔎

## Other options

- **Grab taxi/car** – best late at night or after shopping. See [04-apps.md](04-apps.md).
- **Metered taxi** – insist on the meter ("meter, please"); bad habit of fixed prices near tourist spots. Rama 9 to Siam is typically a few hundred baht depending on traffic.
- **Chao Phraya Express Boat** – scenic way to reach old-town sights.
- **Traffic warning:** Bangkok roads can be gridlocked; at rush hour the train beats a taxi.
