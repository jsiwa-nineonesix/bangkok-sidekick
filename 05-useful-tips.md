# Useful Tips

## Money & payments
- Currency: **Thai Baht (฿ / THB)**. Credit cards work in malls, hotels and many restaurants; **street food, markets, taxis and boats are mostly cash**.
- **MRT accepts contactless Visa/Mastercard at the gate; BTS does not**. See [03-getting-around.md](03-getting-around.md).
- ATMs charge a foreign-card fee (commonly ฿220 per withdrawal) 🔎. Withdraw larger amounts less often.
- Get small notes/coins: ฿20, ฿50, ฿100 and ฿10 coins for BTS machines, markets and tips.
- Currency exchange: SuperRich offices usually have better rates than airports/hotels.
- A SuperRich exchange office is also reported at Central Rama 9 (5th floor), a short walk from the hotel. Confirm on Google Maps or the mall directory before you go 🔎.
- Check with your bank for foreign-transaction fees and travel notices.

## Weather & clothing
- Hot and humid year-round; rainy season heavy downpours are short but intense. Check the forecast for next week. 🔎
- Pack: light clothing, a compact umbrella or rain jacket, sunscreen, sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes.
- Indoors is **very cold A/C**; bring a light layer.
- Temples: cover **shoulders and knees**, no sleeveless tops or short shorts; remove shoes before entering buildings.

## Health & safety
- Drink bottled water; avoid ice from unknown street stalls.
- Pharmacies (Boots, Watsons, local) are common; Central Rama 9 has one.
- Beware pickpockets in crowded markets and on trains; keep phone in a front pocket.
- Motorbike taxis and tuk-tuks: agree on the price before riding if not using an app.
- Crossing the road: traffic does **not** always stop at crossings; use footbridges or cross with locals.
- Rama 9 area is generally safe; use normal city caution at night.
- Common scams: "gem shop" tours, closed-attraction claims, overcharging taxis. Use apps.

## Etiquette
- Greet with a **wai** (palms together) and **"sawasdee krap"** (male) / **"sawasdee ka"** (female).
- Respect the **monarchy**; do not make disparaging comments. Stand when the national anthem plays (8:00 and 18:00 in public places).
- Don't touch people's heads; don't point your feet at people or images of Buddha.
- Remove shoes when entering homes, some shops and temples.
- Keep cool; raising your voice is considered impolite.
- Monks: women should not touch monks or hand things directly to them.

## Connectivity
- **Tourist SIM / eSIM:** AIS, True or dtac. Airports sell SIMs with data; eSIM options also exist. 🔎
- Free Wi-Fi is available in most malls, cafés and the hotel.
- Download offline maps in Google Maps for the Rama 9 area.

Nearby hospitals around Rama 9 include major private hospitals 🔎. Check Google Maps for the nearest one and keep your travel insurance details handy.

## Packing checklist
- Passport and visa info/entry details, hotel booking
- Travel insurance
- Universal adapter (Thailand uses types A/B/C/O at **220 V**) 🔎
- Credit card with **contactless** for the MRT (check it works abroad)
- Cash in small denominations
- Power bank
- Light rain jacket / umbrella
- Sunscreen and insect repellent
- Any prescription medicines (with a copy of the prescription)
