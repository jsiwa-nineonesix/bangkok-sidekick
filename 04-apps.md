# Transport & Delivery Apps

## Install before you land (and sign in with your phone number/email)

| App | Use for | Notes |
|-----|---------|-------|
| **Grab** | Taxis, cars, motorbike taxis, food, parcel | Works in English; pay by card in-app or cash. Show pickup pin carefully (hotel and G Tower are opposite each other, so pick the correct side). |
| **LINE MAN** | Food, groceries, 7-Eleven delivery, motorbike rides, parcel | Thai-first but has English; very popular for restaurant delivery and Thai promo deals. Needs a **LINE account**. Foreign cards sometimes fail – have cash as fallback. 🔎 |
| **MuvMI** | Electric tuk-tuk, short hops (typically within a limited area) | Book in-app, shared rides, fixed fare. Coverage is limited to defined zones; check whether it covers Rama 9 or is only available around Sukhumvit/Ari/Silom. 🔎 |
| **Bolt** | Cheap taxi/cars | Often cheaper than Grab; availability varies |
| **BTS / Rabbit app** | BTS QR tickets and top-ups | Optional |
| **Google Maps** | Navigation, public transit directions, restaurant reviews | Works well offline for saved areas |
| **Google Translate** | Thai menu/sign translation with camera | Download Thai offline pack |
| **LINE** | Chat app; many locals use it | Optional but useful |

## Grab quick tips

1. Set pickup as **hotel entrance** and message the driver in-app if needed.
2. Check the **price shown upfront**; it includes surge pricing during rain or rush hour.
3. **GrabBike** (motorbike taxi) is fastest in traffic but riskier; helmet is provided. Skip if uncomfortable.
4. The driver may call you in Thai – use the in-app chat; it auto-translates.
5. If the driver can't find you, send your live location.
6. Toll roads/expressways: driver may ask you to pay the toll in cash.

## LINE MAN tips

- Address field: type the hotel name, your **room number** and a **local phone number** or reachable messaging app.
- You can order from 7-Eleven, pharmacies and restaurants.
- Delivery fees are low (~฿10–40); extra when raining or far away.
- Rider arrives at the lobby; meet them there.

## MuvMI tips

- Electric three-wheelers, usually shared, seating about 3–4.
- Open the app → choose pickup/drop-off inside the service zone → pay in-app. 🔎
- Great for the "last mile" between a station and a mall or hotel where it operates.

## Pricing guide (rough)

| Ride | Typical cost |
|------|--------------|
| MRT within central zone | ฿17–45 depending on distance 🔎 |
| Grab car, hotel to Siam | ฿150–300 in light traffic, higher at peak 🔎 |
| Motorbike taxi, short hop | ฿20–60 |
| Tuk-tuk (street, negotiated) | Often overpriced for tourists; prefer app rides |

## Avoid

- Taxis that refuse the meter or quote a high flat price.
- Strangers offering "special tours" or saying the Grand Palace is closed today. This is a well-known scam; walk on or check official sources.
- Unlicensed jet-ski or boat deals near tourist spots.
