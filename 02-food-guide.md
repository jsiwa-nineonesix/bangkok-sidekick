# Food Guide – Rama 9 & Nearby

> I haven't verified individual restaurants live, so this guide is organised by **place and type**. Search the name in Google Maps / LINE MAN for current hours, reviews and menus. 🔎 = verify.

## 1. Central Rama 9 (easiest option, air-conditioned)

| Where | What to expect | Budget |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| **Food court / food hall** (check floor on the directory) | Thai dishes, noodles, rice plates, desserts. Buy a **food-court card** at the counter, load it, and refund unused balance before you leave | ฿ |
| **Restaurants floors** | Japanese (sushi, ramen, tonkatsu), Korean BBQ, hot pot / shabu, Thai chains | ฿฿ |
| **Supermarket + Thai snack aisle** | Souvenirs: dried mango, Thai tea, curry paste | ฿ |
| **Cafés** | Coffee and cake when the heat gets too much | ฿฿ |

## 2. Near the hotel / G Tower / Rama 9 Square

- **G Tower lower floors** – lunchtime cafés and quick Thai/Japanese options; popular with office workers, so avoid 12:00–13:00 if you hate queues. 🔎
- **Streets around Rama 9** – small Thai restaurants and street stalls (noodle soup, grilled pork "moo ping", papaya salad "som tam", rice with toppings "khao rad gaeng").
- **7-Eleven / FamilyMart** – open 24h, great for water, toasties, onigiri, snacks.

## 3. Evening / night markets

| Place | Why | Note |
|-------|-----|------|
| **Jodd Fairs Ratchada** | Grilled seafood, spicy salads, mala skewers, drinks, music | Evening only; busy at weekends. The Rama 9 branch has closed 🔎 check opening nights |

## 5. Ordering help

- Not spicy: **"mai phet"** (ไม่เผ็ด). A little spicy: **"phet nit noi"**.
- No peanuts / allergies: show a written note or translate text on your phone. Allergens are often not labelled.
- Bill: **"check bin"** (เช็คบิล) or just wave and say "check bill".
- Tap water isn't for drinking – buy bottled water; restaurants give you filtered water or ice, which is generally safe in established places.
- Tipping isn't expected; ฿20–50 or rounding up is appreciated at restaurants. A 10% service charge is often already added at hotels and nicer restaurants.
