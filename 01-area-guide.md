# Rama 9 / New CBD – Area Guide

## What is it?

Rama 9 (Huai Khwang / Din Daeng district) is Bangkok's **"New CBD"**: modern office towers (G Tower, Rama 9 Square, Grand Rama 9 Tower), condos, hospitals and malls, built around Rama IX Road and Ratchadaphisek Road. It's less touristy than Sukhumvit or Silom, calmer, and good value for food. The **MRT Blue Line** is the main artery.

## Your surroundings (walkable)

| Place | Why go | Getting there |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| **G Tower** | Office tower right opposite your hotel; cafés and food on the lower floors | Across the street |
| **MRT Phra Ram 9 (BL20)** | Your gateway to the whole city | Short walk 🔎 |
| **Central Rama 9** | Large mall: food hall, supermarket, cinema, services, cafés. Rainy-day base | About a 4-minute walk (~250 m), across the road |
| **Rama 9 Square / Grand Rama 9** | Office/commercial cluster with restaurants, convenience stores | Walking distance (~450 m) |
| **Fortune Town** | IT/electronics mall (phones, laptops, gadgets), next to MRT Phra Ram 9 | About a 3-minute walk (~200 m) |

## Downtown highlights (20–40 min)

| Highlight | Notes | How |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| **Grand Palace, Wat Pho, Wat Arun** | Top sights. Dress modestly (covered shoulders/knees). Go early | MRT to Sanam Chai (BL31) for Wat Pho, then walk/ferry 🔎 |
| **Chinatown (Yaowarat)** | Evening street food heaven | MRT Wat Mangkon (BL29) |
| **Siam area (Paragon, Center One, MBK)** | Shopping, air-conditioned malls | BTS Siam |
| **Jim Thompson House** | Thai silk, teak house museum | BTS National Stadium |
| **Lumphini Park** | Green space, monitor lizards, running | MRT Lumphini (BL25) or Silom |
| **ICONSIAM** | Riverside mall with floating market floor | BTS Saphan Taksin, then free shuttle boat |
| **Chatuchak Weekend Market** | Huge market, **Sat–Sun** mainly | MRT Kamphaeng Phet / Chatuchak Park, BTS Mo Chit |
| **Asiatique / Chao Phraya river cruises** | Evening views, dinner cruises | BTS Saphan Taksin + river boat |

> Tip: the Blue Line makes a loop through old town (Hua Lamphong → Sanam Chai → Wat Mangkon), so one train can reach most temples from Rama 9 with a single line.

## Quiet time / downtime ideas near the hotel

- Massage: lots of Thai-massage shops around Rama 9 and Central Rama 9; ฿300–500 per hour is typical for a basic massage. 🔎
- Coffee: cafés in G Tower, Central Rama 9 and the surrounding sois.
- Cinema: Major or SF at nearby malls. English-language films often have English audio plus Thai subtitles.
